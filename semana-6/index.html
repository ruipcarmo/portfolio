Findings

1HighConsistency (local SEO)

Four different opening hours for the same clinic: the site contradicts itself, and both Google Maps and the hidden listing read by Google say something else

Evidence Site footer: Mon–Fri 8:00–13:00 and 14:00–21:00; Sat 8:00–13:00 (08b, 14/11 02:43; same as in week 5, 02/11/2026). Site FAQ page: Mon–Fri 9:00–13:00 and 14:30–21:00; Sat 9:00–14:00 (08, 07/11 05:47). Google Maps listing: Mon–Fri 8:00–20:00; Sat and Sun closed (09, 05:48). Hidden listing (JSON-LD) as read by Google's Rich Results Test: 'Mo,Tu,We,Th,Fr,Sa,Su 09:00-17:00', every day including Sunday (07, 02:47).

How measured Read the footer and the FAQ page; Google Maps → listing → hours (7 days); Rich Results Test → Local businesses → openingHours

Price for the clinic A patient who checks Google Maps believes the clinic is closed on Saturday, while the site says it opens; one who trusts the footer arrives at 8:00 on a Saturday, the FAQ says 9:00. Each mismatch is a lost or annoyed visit. How many: not measurable from outside.

Fix idea Confirm the real hours with the clinic and update, on the same day, the footer, the FAQ page, the Google Business Profile and the hidden listing (SEO plugin settings).

2HighTitle & H1

Neither the Google title nor the main heading says what the clinic does or where: 'Criamos campeões' (We create champions) has no 'fisioterapia' (physiotherapy) and no 'Viseu'

Evidence Title 'Fisioprime - Criamos campeões' (29 characters); one h1, 'Criamos Campeões' (Console, 01, 07/11 05:56). Search 'fisioterapia Viseu' (All tab): fisioprime.pt is on page 2, 3rd result of that page (21, 05:33; the page number is shown in 22, 05:45). Page 1 was not captured, so I do not claim an overall position. On the same page, Doctoralia, just below, has 'fisioterapeutas em Viseu' (physiotherapists in Viseu) in its own title. For this search Google rewrote the grey text instead of using the meta description (21).

How measured Console quick pass; Google search in a private window, counting results without 'Sponsored'; zoom 25% to show the page number

Price for the clinic Anyone searching 'fisioterapia Viseu' who stays on page 1 never sees the site. The title is not proven to be the cause of the page 2 position (Google uses many signals). What is certain: the title is the line a patient reads in the search result before deciding to click, and today it says a slogan, not what the clinic does or where. The owner can change it in 5 minutes. Clicks lost: not measurable from outside (Search Console).

Fix idea Title like 'Fisioterapia em Viseu | FisioPrime – Quinta da Alagoa' (under 60 characters); h1 with the service and the city; keep 'Criamos campeões' as the slogan under it.

3HighSecurity

A machine-written English text about short-term loans, with a link to a payday-loans page, is published on the clinic's WordPress site since 24/02/2021 and listed in the map of pages sent to Google: the typical pattern of spam injected into a site

Evidence post-sitemap.xml lists 1 address, last modified 2021-02-24 (04, 07/11 03:20). The page opens with the FisioPrime menu and a link to badcreditloanmart.com (05, 03:21). The text reads as machine-spun, e.g. 'charge curiosity about' where 'interest' is meant (05). Its robots setting is 'index' (open to Google). A search 'site:fisioprime.pt loans' returns no results (06, 03:20). Still in place on 16/11/2026 (mentor's re-check).

How measured Open the sitemap index and its 'post' part; open the article and read it; Google search with site:

Price for the clinic A patient or partner who lands on it would question who looks after the site. Google does not show it today for 'loans'; the real question is who can publish on the site. Someone who could add this page in 2021 may still have access. Raise it privately with the owner, as two questions: 'Did you know this page exists?' and 'Who has administrator access to the site?'

Fix idea Ask the clinic (or the agency that maintains the site) to review who has administrator access, change the passwords, remove the page, check for other unknown pages and update WordPress and its plugins; keep the sitemap to the clinic's real pages.

4MediumMeta description & team

The text Google shows under the title promises a team the site doesn't show: 'quatro Fisioterapeutas e um Nutricionista' (four physiotherapists and one nutritionist)

Evidence Meta description (160 characters) used word for word by Google when searching 'fisioprime' (11, 02:49). The site shows 4 people: 3 physiotherapists (Silvana Ferreira, Alexandra Couto, Gustavo Almeida) and 1 nutritionist (Marco Pontes) (Console h2 list; 10, 05:49). Team carousel: 5 frames, one empty with only the logo (10). Clicking Silvana Ferreira opens her profile (10b, 05:58); clicking Alexandra Couto opens nothing (10c, 05:58). CHANGED SINCE: on 16/11/2026 the mentor's re-check found Alexandra Couto no longer on the site (home page and /equipa/); the team shows 2 physiotherapists and 1 nutritionist, while the description still promises four (not re-measured by me).

How measured Console: meta description and h2 list; Google search for the name; click test comparing a profile that opens with one that doesn't

Price for the clinic The first sentence a future patient reads about the clinic on Google is not what the site shows: small, but it is the first impression. An empty frame and a profile that doesn't open make the team page look unfinished.

Fix idea Rewrite the description with what is true today and an invitation to book (e.g. 'Fisioterapia e nutrição em Viseu, na Quinta da Alagoa. Marque a sua avaliação: 964 413 656.'); remove the empty frame; show the team as it is today. (Not proposed: fixing the profile that did not open — a profile that stops opening often means someone is leaving; check with the clinic first.)

5MediumGEO (AI answers)

Google's AI answer for 'fisioprime' presents a clinic in Rio de Janeiro first; the Viseu clinic only appears in passing

Evidence AI Overview, search 'fisioprime': opens with 'Fisioprime Campo Grande… Rio de Janeiro', with its address and phone; Portugal and 'FisioPrime in Viseu' only in the 2nd bullet (12, 07/11 03:14). No llms.txt (404, Console, 01). The clinic's name is not written the same everywhere: 'FisioPrime Viseu' on Google Maps (09), 'Fisioprime' in the title and in the hidden listing (01, 07).

How measured Google search for the name in a private window; read the AI Overview to the end; Console: /llms.txt

Price for the clinic Someone who asks Google (or an AI assistant) about the clinic by name first gets a Brazilian clinic's phone number. Calls or visits lost: not measurable from outside. AI answers change over time.

Fix idea Use one name everywhere ('FisioPrime Viseu'); add 'Viseu' to the title, h1 and description; add an llms.txt and complete the hidden listing (city, phone, Google Maps link) so machines can tell this clinic from its namesakes.

6MediumSpeed (mobile)

On mobile the main content takes 7.1 s to appear in Google's test, almost three times the 2.5 s 'good' limit

Evidence PageSpeed Insights, Mobile: Performance 69; Largest Contentful Paint 7.1 s; Speed Index 6.0 s; Total Blocking Time 0 ms; layout shift 0.001 (14, 07/11 03:12). Desktop: 90; LCP 1.4 s (13, 03:11). Real-user data: 'No Data' (15). Measured on Google's servers, not on my connection.

How measured pagespeed.web.dev → Mobile and Desktop tabs

Price for the clinic Most people look for a clinic on their phone; for 7 seconds they see an unfinished page. How many leave: not measurable from outside (no real-user data).

Fix idea In PageSpeed, open 'Largest Contentful Paint element' to see which piece is slow (likely the hero image) and compress or resize it; the week 5 fixes for weight (WebP images, fewer style files) also apply.

7MediumImages (alt text)

Almost half the images still have no text description for Google and screen readers (already reported in week 5)

Evidence 24 of 49 images without alt text (Console, 01, 07/11 05:56). Week 5: 24 of 50 (02/11/2026). CHANGED SINCE: 18 of 48 on 16/11/2026 (mentor's re-check, not re-measured by me).

How measured Console quick pass after scrolling to the end of the page

Price for the clinic Google Images can't tell what the photos show, and blind visitors hear nothing; no direct loss of bookings.

Fix idea Describe every meaningful photo (team, treatments); leave decorative images empty.

8MediumLocal SEO (reviews)

Same 4.9★ as the leaders, but only 10 Google reviews: the first clinic on the map has 124

Evidence 'fisioterapia Viseu', Places list: MoviMente Viseu 4.9★ (124), FisioPrime Viseu 4.9★ (10), Carolina Aparício 4.9★ (64) (16, 07/11 02:48; 17, 03:22).

How measured Google search 'fisioterapia Viseu' in a private window; read the Places list

Price for the clinic With equal stars, a patient comparing listings trusts 124 opinions more than 10. Effect on map position: not provable from outside.

Fix idea Ask satisfied patients for a review at the last session (a QR code at reception linking to the listing).

Strengths

  • robots.txt lets search engines in and points to the sitemap (02)
  • Sitemap with 4 parts; the pages part was updated on 25/10/2026 (03)
  • A hidden listing (JSON-LD) exists and Google reads it as a Local business (07) — but with the wrong hours
  • One h1 on the home page; 24 internal links reach every menu page (01)
  • Desktop speed 90, LCP 1.4 s; no layout shift (0.006 / 0.001) and 0 ms blocking time on both (13, 14)
  • PageSpeed SEO 100 and Best Practices 100 (15) — the automatic test checks that the pieces exist, not that they are true
  • Google Maps: 2nd or 3rd for 'fisioterapia Viseu' in 4 measurements, excluding the ad (16–19), 4.9★

Google's text and Google's AI

What I could not measure from outside

  • Clicks, impressions and real search terms are only in Google Search Console (owner only)
  • No real-user speed data ('No Data'): only Google's lab test
  • Searches made from Timor-Leste in a private window; Google set the area to 'Viseu, Portugal', but a person in Viseu may see a different order
  • Why the site is on page 2 cannot be proven from outside: Google uses many signals
  • AI answers change over time; the screenshot shows 07/11/2026 only
  • Number of bookings lost to each finding: only the clinic knows

Prices are given as consequences, not euros: the clinic's visits and bookings are only visible to the clinic.
