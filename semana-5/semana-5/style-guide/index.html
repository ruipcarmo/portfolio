1 · Name ideas

Five names, one pick

Each name is short, easy to say in Portuguese and English, and comes from one idea: giving businesses their time back. Before choosing, check that the domain and social handles are free and that no company nearby already uses the name.

My pick
tempo.

Time is the most valuable currency a business has. The name says exactly what the agency gives back.

Option 2
Passo a Passo

“Step by step”: no jargon, no rush. Warm and trustworthy, best for clients in Portugal.

Option 3
Clareira

A clearing in the forest: space and light where there was clutter. Links to nature and to clear explanations.

Option 4
Atalho

“Shortcut”. Says what the agency does: finds the faster way to do the same work.

Option 5
Ritmo Lab

“Rhythm”. A business that runs smoothly has a good rhythm; “Lab” says we test and improve.

How I chose

Short (1–2 syllables to say). Same meaning in PT and EN. Tells a story about time. Not tied to one kind of client.

7 · Rules

Do and don’t

Taken from the audits of real local websites in this module.

Lead with a promise

The biggest words say what the client gains, not who we are.

One green button

A clear verb (“Book”, “Call”) and visible without scrolling on a phone.

Real proof, near the top

Real numbers and real work. Nothing invented.

No stock photos

Show real projects, real people or nothing at all.

No third font

Figtree and Instrument Serif only.

No centred walls of text

Align left; one idea per paragraph.

8 · Voice

Short. Clear. With weight.

Tempo speaks in short sentences that end with a full stop that matters. Two beats, then silence. No jargon, no exclamation marks.

Brand line · About page
Dinheiro recupera-se. Tempo não.

“Money comes back. Time doesn’t.” Followed by: «Com base nesta ideia, nasceu este projeto.» (“This project was born from that idea.”) The brand speaks for itself: the founder’s name appears only in the legal footer and on invoices.

Two beats

“Save time. Stay ahead.” Two short sentences beat one long one.

Play with the name

“tempo” (time) and “Tempo” (the brand) can share a sentence.

No invented team

“We” is the voice of the brand. Never fake people, titles or numbers.
