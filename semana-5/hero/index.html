What changed and why

Issue 1 · Hierarchy

A promise instead of “Início” (Home)

Before: the largest texts were the name (46 px) and “Início” (34 px). Neither said what the clinic does.

After: the main heading is a short promise: “Menos dor. Mais movimento.” (Less pain. More movement.) The service and location sit in the small label above it.

Issues 2 and 3 · Opening text

One useful line instead of 3 paragraphs

Before: a generic welcome and 100 words about a medicine, linking to its sales page at an online pharmacy. At 375 px it took up 491 px.

After: one short line built on the logo’s tagline: “Fisioterapia, saúde e bem-estar, num plano feito à sua medida.” (Physiotherapy, health and wellbeing, in a plan tailored to you.) No commercial link.

Issue 5 · Social proof

Reviews move up to the first screen

Before: 6 reviews from 2023 at the bottom of the page, with no average or total.

After: 5.0 ★ · 35 Google reviews right below the buttons, still on the first screen, linking to the current reviews.

Issues 4 and 6 · Booking button

A big button that actually works

Before: “Pedir marcação” (Book) was 30 px tall in the sidebar; on mobile it only appeared on the 2nd screen; the form did not send requests.

After: “Marcar avaliação” (Book an assessment), 54 px tall, in the only accent colour, on the first screen. It opens WhatsApp with the message pre-written. Next to it, “Ligar” (Call).

Issue 7 · Images

Space reserved for a real photo

Before: 0 photos on the home page.

After: a large space for a photo of the team or the clinic, waiting for the clinic’s permission. No stock photos used.

Style guide

Rules from the benchmark (movimente.pt)

2 typefaces (Figtree and Fraunces). 1 action colour, reserved for the booking button. Left-aligned text with room to breathe. Touch targets of at least 46 px.
